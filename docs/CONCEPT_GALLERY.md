## Design direction

- Two physically distinct shops: clothing outlet conversion and separate jewelry shop in the adjacent parking lot.
- Street-inspired and believable for Grove Street, while clean and professionally finished.
- Independent names, sign textures, and accent colors for each business.
- Optimization-first construction: modular geometry, restrained material count, sensible texture sizes, simple collision, and no unnecessary duplicate surfaces.
